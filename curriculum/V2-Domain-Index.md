## Recommended learning sequence

1. **Weeks 1–4 — Gap audit:** review A–J, prioritizing gaps in C#, SQL tuning, API security, and messaging. Build a small API + LLM + UI vertical slice early.
2. **Months 2–4 — Python, math, and ML:** focus on AE, P, and Q; work on K–L and practice DSA; begin the first capstone.
3. **Months 4–7 — LLMs, RAG, and frameworks:** study R, S, T, and AA; strengthen M and N; deliver a capstone with an evaluation set.
4. **Months 7–10 — Agents and production AI:** study U, V, W, and Z; deliver a workflow with approval gates and auditability.
5. **Months 10–13 — Platform, cloud, and data:** deepen O and study AB as needed; deploy an AI platform with CI/CD, infrastructure as code, and a cost report.
6. **Months 13–15 — Architect readiness:** finish X, AC, and Y; prepare design documents, mock interviews, certification study, and an evidence pack.

Treat the month ranges as a flexible plan. The curriculum suggests roughly 12–15 months at about 10 study hours per week; adjust priorities to your role and available time.

## Completion evidence

For each major subject or project, capture what you can explain and demonstrate: working code, tests, edge and failure cases, security and reliability choices, a diagram or ADR, and a demo or concise walkthrough.
